How to use this site

Use it as a personal awareness guide, not as legal advice. The focus is practical privacy literacy: knowing what systems collect, where risk accumulates, and which everyday habits reduce unnecessary exposure.

Project idea

This repo is intentionally different from the LPR map. It avoids uncertain pin locations and instead becomes a polished privacy education app that can grow with new cards, quizzes, and resource links.
